Before you start

Each card looks at one Wikipedia article and every study on it that others have tried to replicate (from FORRT's database of replication attempts). An AI model read the article and the papers we could get, and suggested what, if anything, should change. How to use it: read the card, check the sources, make any edit on Wikipedia itself in another tab, then come back and record what you did.

  • The AI wording is not for pasting. English Wikipedia bans AI-written article text, and other editors may collapse talk-page comments that are obviously AI-written. Read the sources and write your own text.
  • "No change needed" is a common, useful answer. A failed replication does not make a sentence wrong: many articles cite a study for its history or already discuss the replication.
  • Start with "Adds a replication". Our main aim is to bring replication evidence into articles that lack it. The first tab lists the cards whose suggested edit would cite a replication the article does not cite yet. Other corrections we noticed along the way are useful but secondary; they are under the other tabs.
  • Labels. "Edit suggested" means the article says something its source does not support, or leaves out a replication that bears on a claim it makes. "Optional addition" means the article is accurate and the extra sentence is up to you. "Judgement call" means two independent drafting runs disagreed. "Minor notes" means no change to the text is suggested, but the card lists small things such as a reference typo or material that belongs in another article.
  • Cards are snapshots. Use "Changes since this snapshot" or "Check now" to see whether someone has already edited the article.
  • "What we checked" lists the facts the model verified against the papers, each with the quoted passage. Your part is the editorial judgement and writing, not re-checking facts; but if a quote looks wrong, say so in your notes.
  • Reviews outweigh single studies. One replication can support a short, attributed sentence; claims about "the evidence" need a review. Take extra care with living people and health topics (flagged). If unsure, raise it on the talk page or mark "needs more checking".
  • Your records stay in this browser. Add your initials in the toolbar and use Export CSV or Export JSON to save or share them.
