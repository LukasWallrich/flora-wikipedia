Of 422 unique original studies detected on English Wikipedia, 54 (12.8%) had at least one page containing a replication DOI. This is a percentage of originals, not of individual citations. The cards identify pairs without a detected replication DOI for human review. Replications may be acknowledged through DOI-free references or secondary sources.
