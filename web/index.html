Replication evidence in Wikipedia citations

Wikipedia often cites studies that have since been replicated. This project uses FLoRA, the FORRT Library of Replication Attempts, to find those citations on English Wikipedia and check whether the article also cites the replication. Two versions of the May 2026 pilot analysis are kept here.

The Replication Gap

The original pilot analysis: 422 replicated studies cited on English Wikipedia, with the articles where the replication is not cited, ordered by replication outcome.

The same data, read more cautiously

An October 2026 revision of that page. It corrects how the headline percentage is described and presents the list as cases for human review, because a missing replication DOI does not show that an article ignores the evidence.
